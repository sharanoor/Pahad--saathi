Mountain Safety Tips

Remember these simple habits before and during your journey.

🌦️ Check the weatherAsk a parent or teacher about heavy rain or official warnings before leaving.
👟 Walk carefullyWear suitable shoes, avoid running on steep or slippery paths, and stay away from edges.
⛰️ Stay away from dangerNever go close to a fresh landslide, falling rocks, damaged bridges or flooded streams.
📱 Tell a trusted adultShare your route and expected arrival time with a parent, guardian or teacher.
🚫 Never cross floodwaterWater can be deeper or faster than it looks. Turn back and seek safe guidance.
📢 Follow official adviceListen to local authorities and school instructions. Do not spread unverified alerts.

Need Help?

⚠️ If there is immediate danger

Move away from the danger if you can do so safely. Do not investigate landslides or cross floodwater. Tell a parent, teacher or trusted adult and follow local official instructions.

In India, 112 is the national emergency number. Call it for an immediate emergency when safe to do so; do not rely on this website as an emergency service.

About this project

Pahad Saathi is a student-built prototype for safety education. The quiz works in the browser. Demo reports are stored only on the current device/browser and are not shared. The project does not provide live weather, location tracking, official reporting or emergency alerts.
